Horizon 100

Room to come together. Space to call your own.

Concept in development

Horizon 100 explores a spacious family home arranged in three wings around a central gathering space. The concept pairs containers side by side in each of the two private wings to create wider rooms, with shared living at the heart of the home.

It imagines a home that opens towards its surroundings, with outdoor spaces for quiet mornings, shared meals and time with family or friends.

Generous living. Thoughtful use of resources.

As Horizon 100 develops, our aim is to combine spacious family living with considered material choices, opportunities for reuse and attention to the surrounding site. Environmental performance will inform the design as its structure, materials and systems are refined.

Modular Project Concepts

Spaces shaped around people and purpose.

Beyond individual homes, modular design opens possibilities for places to live, work, receive care and welcome guests. These concepts illustrate how container-based spaces could be arranged around different needs.

Community & Supportive Housing

A place to belong. Space to regain stability.

Private accommodation arranged around shared kitchens, dining areas and community facilities.

This concept explores longer-term housing for people unable to afford a home, transitional accommodation while individuals and families regain financial stability, and temporary housing following disasters.

Projects could be developed with governments, NGOs or community organisations, with funding, eligibility, management and support services established with the sponsoring organisation.

Healthcare & Veterinary Clinics

Spaces designed around care.

Modular concepts for human healthcare or veterinary services, with consultation, treatment and supporting spaces planned around each use. Layouts, equipment and requirements would be developed with the relevant professionals and project partners.

Hospitality Concepts

A distinctive place to stay.

Explore possibilities for boutique accommodation and holiday rentals, combining private guest spaces with shared dining, leisure or reception areas according to the project.

Modular Offices

Room to work together.

Office concepts bringing reception, meeting rooms and private workspaces into a coordinated setting, with covered connections and supporting facilities shaped around the organisation’s needs.
