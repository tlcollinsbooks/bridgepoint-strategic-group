Our Role

We guide. We do not lend.

Azuero Capital Advisory is not a bank, lender or financial institution. We assist clients by helping them prepare financing applications and connect with accredited banking partners. Final approval depends entirely on the client’s financial position and the bank’s lending criteria.

Important Information

BridgePoint does not provide loans, approve applications or guarantee financing. All lending decisions are made solely by the participating financial institution.
